<i>ThystTV</i> is a third-party <a href='https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitch_(service)' target='_blank'>Twitch</a> client for Android, focused on player polish, floating chat, local viewing stats, and large-screen comfort. It is a fork of <a href='https://github.com/crackededed/Xtra' target='_blank'>Xtra</a>.

What ThystTV adds on top of the base client:

* Floating chat overlay for full-screen viewing — keep chat visible without a cramped split layout
* Gesture-based playback controls for seeking, playback speed, brightness, and volume
* Custom playback quality and speed dialogs, with duration-aware VoD scrubbing
* Local watch-history and screen-time stats: category breakdowns, viewing patterns, favorite channels — all stored only on your device
* Layouts tuned for phones, tablets, and wide screens
* Optional in-app update checks against GitHub releases, with rendered changelogs (off by default)

Base features inherited from Xtra:

* Support for popular emotes with BetterTTV, FrankerFaceZ, and 7TV
* Watch VODs and clips with chat replay
* Download VODs to watch offline
* Picture-in-Picture mode and background audio
* Sleep timer

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Stats stay local on the device.
