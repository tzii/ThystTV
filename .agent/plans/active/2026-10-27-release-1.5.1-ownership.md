# Correct 1.5.1 Cast request ownership before publication

## Goal

Close or replace a player without reviving delayed receiver loads, disconnecting a newer source, or stopping retained media a new screen never acquired. Rebuild and review a new exact signed 1.5.1 candidate, then publish and update issues #60/#61.

## Non-goals

No broad player refactor, upstream sync, unrelated cleanup or signing changes.

## Risks

Risk level: high. Cancellation must retain the configured source factory for ordinary local preparation, while retiring delayed lookups and fallback snapshots. Pending-screen cleanup must stop its own accepted source even when its acknowledgement is delayed, without touching unrelated media. A source change invalidates the signed RC and requires renewed gates.

## Human approval

Required before implementation: yes; received in the requests to finish and release 1.5.1. Fixing confirmed release blockers is within that scope. Publication authorization does not claim unperformed exact-byte device QA.

## Implementation steps

1. Verify each finding against actual source and preserve the raw attributed review.
2. Cancel pending handoff work on explicit stop/clear/close and count pending lookups in the alternate-backend guard.
3. Bind deferred fallback disconnects to the current source/route request.
4. Record source acceptance, and make close requests conditional on a unique accepted service request so pending/rejected screens cannot stop unrelated media.
5. Add delayed-callback and pending-acknowledgement regression coverage, update QA docs, run required Gradle gates and inspect the diff.
6. Open a narrow follow-up PR, merge after green CI, freeze a new master SHA, build/verify a replacement signed bundle and repeat the required independent reviews and exact-byte QA before publication.
